# Related Expression

Related Expressions are shown in the top-left corner of the card, you can click them to visit the card immediately.
You can fill the **RelatedExpression** field with any comma-separated expression.
If this field is left empty, the two newest cards from the notes query will be picked instead.

</video>

:::tip

- Expressions that are explicitly included in the **RelatedExpression** field will be underlined.
- You can use comma `,` `、` or semicolons `;` `；` as delimiters.

:::
